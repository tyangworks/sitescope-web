# Growth positioning

SiteScope is ONE integrated product composed of sitescope-web and sitescope-crawler.

H1: Find What's Hurting Your Website Growth.
Support: Audit visibility, performance, content, and conversions across traditional search (SEO) and AI search (GEO). Get a prioritized fix plan.

Public navigation: Analyze, Pricing, Content, Services. Login becomes Account after verified session lookup; Reports/History appears for signed-in users. Get Free Audit remains the primary action. SEO/GEO never become top-level navigation items.

Four areas: Search Visibility (SEO + GEO readiness), Performance, Content & Messaging, Conversion. Preserve current dark/teal/blue design. Do not promise traffic, rankings, citation probability, or fixed turnaround. Overall score remains a heuristic, distinct from deterministic SEO/GEO readiness.

Free/Pro/Custom remain the only tiers. Pro displays $9 with $29 struck through, one-time. Support stays secondary in footer/report end. No donation can unlock Pro.
